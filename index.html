How to play

Drag the cue ball from the holder into the D. Aim with the pointer or A / D. Pull back and release to shoot, or choose power and select Shoot.

Pot a red, then a colour. After the reds, clear yellow through black. The computer checks angles and obstacles before choosing a shot. Practice tools enable force-potting by clicking a ball.

This is a simplified rules simulation. First-contact and full tournament foul rules are not enforced. Keyboard shortcuts: 1 / 2 / 3 change layout; M toggles practice.
